About Us

Empowering Students, Building Careers

Who We Are

The Training and Placement Cell bridges the gap between academic learning and professional careers. We help students develop the skills, confidence, and knowledge needed to succeed in the corporate world.

🎯

Our Vision

To empower students with the skills and confidence needed to achieve their career goals.

🚀

Our Mission

To provide quality training, career guidance, and placement opportunities for every student.

🎓

Skill Development

To improve technical skills, communication, aptitude, and interview performance.

💼

Placements

To connect students with companies through campus recruitment and internship opportunities.

What We Do

We organize aptitude training, mock interviews, group discussions, technical workshops, internships, and campus recruitment drives to make students industry-ready.
